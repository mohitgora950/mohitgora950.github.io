Daily use: Personal Vault App

My own everyday app, now live on the web and installable on Android, iPhone and desktop. One secure place for links, screenshots, PDFs, important documents, passwords, notes, a drawing diary and date-wise to-dos, with search that finds everything.

  • Encrypted vault: passwords, documents and diary are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from the login password; logins are hashed with bcrypt and rate-limited.
  • Paste anything: Ctrl+V a screenshot, link or text anywhere and the app asks where to file it. Link titles (e.g. YouTube) are fetched automatically.
  • Smart search across notes, links, files, PDFs, folders and tasks, tolerant of typos and Hinglish shortcuts (yt, ss).
  • A4 diary with shapes, arrays and arrows, encrypted PDF export, plus Images → PDF, an AI assistant and per-user Google Drive storage.
  • Installable PWA with app shortcuts, an offline page and Android Share → Daily use; deployed on Vercel + Neon PostgreSQL.

AI × Cloud
Featured · 2026

Cloud-Native LLM Command-Parser Microservice

A production-style setup for serving a language model. I distilled a large teacher LLM into a 70M-parameter student model that turns plain-English commands into structured JSON, then deployed it on Kubernetes with autoscaling, zero-downtime rollouts and automated CI/CD.

  • Wrote sharded parallel workers to generate teacher labels, and a CPU-optimised sequence-level distillation trainer.
  • Kept the container image under 2 GB (1.7 GB) using CPU-only PyTorch wheels; model weights load from S3 (SeaweedFS) when a pod starts.
  • Kubernetes setup: Traefik ingress, HPA (70% CPU target), resource limits, network ACLs and subnets.
  • GitHub Actions CI/CD with secret scanning (Gitleaks), linting, PyTest and a model-quality gate.

EduRate: Professor Rating & Analytics Platform

A full-stack platform for IIIT Manipur with three roles (Student, Professor and Admin). Students rate professors and raise doubts, professors see their feedback, and admins get an analytics dashboard with predicted performance and automated email reports.

  • Secure auth with JWT + bcryptjs and role-based access control across the app.
  • A performance predictor that combines a weighted scoring formula with regression.
  • Admin analytics built with Chart.js, plus automated email reports through Nodemailer.
  • A doubt system with rate limits (max 2 per day) and an abusive-language filter to keep feedback respectful.
  • Normalised MySQL schema with SQL views for admin reporting, served by a REST API.

AI · 2026

Real-Time Sign Language Translator

Recognises hand gestures from a webcam, shows them as text and speaks them aloud, fully offline. The aim is to make communication easier for people who sign.

  • Pipeline: OpenCV capture → MediaPipe HandLandmarker (21 landmarks) → classifier that accounts for orientation and left/right hand → speech.
  • A custom vocabulary of up to 256 pose combinations (5 finger states × 4 orientations × 2 hands).
  • Per-finger hysteresis and majority-vote smoothing remove flicker; a gesture is confirmed after about 0.1 s of stability.
  • Speech runs in the background and can be interrupted; webcam-free unit tests cover the logic. Next step: a trained PyTorch model.
